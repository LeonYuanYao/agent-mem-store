# Use incremental Weekly and paginated Monthly governance

Weekly Governance processes changes since its last successful cursor and a bounded affected relationship neighborhood. It consolidates duplicate or overlapping Agent-derived knowledge across Batches and Sessions, updates supported relationship proposals, checks recently stale applicability, evaluates corroborated Global Candidates, and performs only already-authorized Agent-derived merge, supersede, or archive actions. Human-authored conflict or suspected staleness creates a Verification Request instead of an automatic edit.

Monthly Governance performs all Weekly duties when the schedules coincide and adds a restartable paginated audit across all Active and Archived metadata and the relationship graph. It examines orphaned or broken relationships, duplicate clusters, time-sensitive confirmation debt, applicability drift, Project and Global generalization, high-value inflation, irrelevant Bad Cases, retrieval distribution, index health, Luna incident trends, and governance backlog.

Monthly may identify restore, archive, and future purge obligations and emits one bounded Monthly Governance Summary plus aggregated Review Inbox items. It cannot silently change Human-authored Memory, model or adapter, ranking threshold, scope, schedule, or safety policy.

At this historical milestone, the Engineering MVP recorded archive-body purge obligations but did not execute body purge. A later reviewed milestone implemented deletion; ADR-0012, ADR-0079, and ADR-0085 are authoritative for the current three-calendar-month retention and executor behavior.
