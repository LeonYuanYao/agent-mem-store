# Require anchored score five after the epoch soft target

After automatic injection reaches the 8,192-token Context Epoch soft target, a previously uninjected Memory revision requires an explainable relevance score of at least five and at least one primary anchor. A primary anchor is direct Memory identity reference; exact file, symbol, error code, command, or entity match; or very-strong semantic match combined with explicit applicability or a bounded Session-signal match. A one-hop relationship and broad topic similarity cannot satisfy this requirement.

Eligible authority and evidence are Human-authored Memory, explicitly human-confirmed Agent-derived Memory, or verified Agent-derived Memory whose effective SessionStart tier is `critical` or `strong`. Ordinary `normal` Agent-derived Memory remains available through Explicit Deep Retrieval but cannot use automatic post-soft capacity merely by accumulating relevance points.

Post-soft automatic injection uses compact representation only, admits at most two items per UserPromptSubmit, and retains the once-per-Memory-revision-per-epoch rule. Remaining space before the 12,288-token hard limit does not authorize standard upgrades or any eligibility bypass.

The score-five threshold and two-item allowance are configurable first-version defaults. Shadow Mode records qualifying, blocked, explicitly deep-read, relevant, and irrelevant outcomes and may recommend changes, but active values require explicit human Review.
