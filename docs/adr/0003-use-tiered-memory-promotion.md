# Use tiered memory promotion

Human-authored Memory is the highest content authority within its applicable scope and must not be silently overwritten by automation. Agent-derived candidates may become Durable Memories automatically only when they are explicit, traceable, directly evidenced or successfully verified, clearly scoped, non-conflicting, and neither sensitive nor transient. Other candidates remain outside normal recall until later evidence, governance, or human review resolves them.

This policy preserves background capture without granting every model inference the authority of established knowledge. Incorrect or prohibited content may be rejected instead of being retained merely for possible future cleanup.
